titolo: Gnocchi con la fioreta
sottotitolo: The De.Co. flagship product of Recoaro Terme
storia: >-
  «Al scoro che resta dopo aver fato el formaio, ghe vienn xontà el sale amaro
  o el caio vecio e tuto fato boere. Dopo un poco vien su la fioreta.» In the old
  dialect: to what is left at the bottom of the cauldron after making cheese,
  add coarse salt or old rennet and bring it all to the boil; after a while the
  fioreta rises. Fioreta is ricotta in its liquid state, the cream that surfaces
  while cow's milk is being worked in the malghe. It is not an ingredient you
  buy: it is what remains of an old gesture, and that is why gnocchi with
  fioreta are made only where the malghe still exist. Recoaro Terme, which in
  the Middle Ages was called Requaro — from the Cimbrian words rech and quar,
  muddy ground — is the place where this preparation took root. On the first
  Sunday of September the tradition is renewed with the Festa dei gnocchi con la
  fioreta.
ricetta:
  - ingrediente: Fioreta
    quantita: 1.5 litres
  - ingrediente: White flour
    quantita: 1 kg
  - ingrediente: Malga butter
    quantita: 300 g
  - ingrediente: Aged malga cheese
    quantita: 300 g
  - ingrediente: Salt
    quantita: to taste
lavorazione:
  - titolo: The mixture
    testo: >-
      The fioreta is worked with the flour until it becomes a semi-liquid
      batter with no lumps. You can tell the consistency by eye: it should drop
      from the spoon, not run.
  - titolo: From scratch
    testo: >-
      The mixture is dropped by the spoonful into boiling water. The gnocchi
      rise on their own when they are cooked.
  - titolo: Ready-made
    testo: >-
      The «della Nonna Maria» version needs 6-8 minutes of boiling.
condimento: >-
  300 g of malga butter browned until hazelnut-coloured, with aged malga cheese
  or smoked ricotta.
riconoscimenti:
  - Denominazione Comunale di Origine (municipal designation of origin) — Comune di Recoaro Terme
