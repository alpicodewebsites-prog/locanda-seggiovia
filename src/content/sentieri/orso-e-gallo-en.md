nome: Sentiero dell'Orso e del Gallo
ordine: 7
sommario: >-
  Old tracks among the hamlets on the sunny side above Recoaro Terme, with the
  glimpses of the Piccole Dolomiti.
descrizione: >-
  It climbs from the centre of Recoaro along the left-hand side of the Agno
  valley and crosses the hamlets on the sunny side: Menarini, Asnicar, Tallera,
  Orsati. In between come the Suntra woods and the Baita del Toni Pata, known as
  «l'orso», the bear — and from that the name. The return can come down by the
  Sentiero del Gallo, passing the Trattoria alla Gabiola. This is the route that
  starts from the town with no drive first: you leave the car and begin to
  climb.
