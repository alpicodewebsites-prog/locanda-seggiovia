sommario: >-
  A linear route from the lower slopes of Recoaro Mille to Rifugio Cesare
  Battisti alla Gazza, among centuries-old beeches, birches and ashes.
descrizione: >-
  The path crosses Malga Morando and the Trattoria La Gabiola, climbing from the
  lower slopes of Recoaro Mille up to Rifugio Cesare Battisti alla Gazza. The
  name comes from what you meet on the way: beeches, birches and ashes that have
  more years than anyone looking at them. Along the route the views open onto
  the Carega group. It cannot be walked with a pushchair.
